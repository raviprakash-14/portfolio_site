About Me

Builder at
Heart

Hi, I’m Raviprakash K — a builder, technology enthusiast and startup-minded creator who enjoys turning ideas into working digital products.

Most of my time goes into AI and AI-powered applications, automation, frontend and software product work, UI/UX, and the immersive side of things like AR and VR. I like the part where a rough idea becomes something you can open, click and actually use — so I tend to learn by building, shipping and then taking the result apart again.

Alongside my own projects I’m a co-founder at Ornalens, which is where a lot of my product and startup experience comes from. Experimentation is the through-line: new tools, new stacks, and finding out where creative technology can go next.
